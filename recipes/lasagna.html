Lasagna Recipe

This is a delicious lasagna recipe.

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ground beef
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  3. In a skillet, cook the ground beef until browned.
  4. Layer the noodles, meat, tomato sauce, and cheeses in a baking dish.
  5. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used.
  6. Bake for 45 minutes or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  7. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving.
