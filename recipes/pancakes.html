Pancakes Recipe

This is a delicious pancakes recipe.

  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Baking powder
  • Salt
  • Butter

Instructions:

  1. In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, whisk milk and eggs together.
  3. Combine wet and dry ingredients, then stir in melted butter.
  4. Heat a skillet over medium heat and pour batter to form pancakes.
  5. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook until golden brown.
  6. Serve with your favorite toppings.
